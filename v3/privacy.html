Privacy Policy

ProformConnect  ·  Last updated: July 28, 2026

What This Policy Covers

This policy describes how ProformConnect ("we," "us") handles information collected through this website. This website is an informational site with a contact form. It does not provide account access, platform login, or any other interactive service.

What We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide. We do not collect information beyond what you voluntarily submit.

Our web server may automatically log standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used for basic site operation and is not used to identify or track individual visitors.

How We Use It

We use the information you submit through the contact form to respond to your inquiry. That's it.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use it to serve advertising.

Cookies and Tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. Any cookies used are limited to basic site functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to request that we delete information you've submitted, use the contact form on this site.
