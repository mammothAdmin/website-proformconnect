What We Do

Three services. One team.

Mass tort case development requires extensive client interaction. When clients don't respond or provide incomplete information, cases stall and revenue is lost.

Intake

Receive and process client inquiries through structured intake forms. Automated first-pass triage with human review for complex cases, followed by warm handoff to the matched attorney or firm.

  • Structured intake forms
  • Qualification and triage
  • Warm handoff to matched firm
  • Escalation rules and SLA tracking

Client Outreach

Proactive, professional engagement that keeps clients informed and responsive. When a client goes quiet, our team follows up before the case goes dark, preventing the silent attrition that erodes portfolios.

  • Follow-up on non-responsive clients
  • Status updates and check-ins
  • Reassignment escalation
  • Attrition prevention

Document Assistance

Guide clients through plaintiff fact sheets, long-form questionnaires, and document collection. Every field validated against screening data, every response documented accurately.

  • Plaintiff fact sheet completion
  • Long-form questionnaire interviews
  • Information validation
  • Missing document follow-up
