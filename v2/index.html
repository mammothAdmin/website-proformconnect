What We Do

Three services. One dedicated team.

When client communication falls behind, cases stall and revenue slips away. We handle the follow-up, the paperwork, and the outreach — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Intake

We receive and qualify new client inquiries on your behalf. Structured screening, warm handoffs to the right attorney, and clear tracking from first contact to case assignment.

Client Outreach

When clients go quiet, we follow up — professionally and persistently. Regular check-ins, status updates, and re-engagement calls that keep cases alive and clients informed.

Document Assistance

We guide clients through questionnaires, fact sheets, and document collection. Every response validated, every gap followed up on, every form completed accurately.
